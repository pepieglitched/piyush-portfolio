[Project notes](/projects/tridix/)

**Status:** Built

**Purpose:** Physics concepts can be difficult to grasp through written explanations and flat diagrams alone. Tridix was created to make them more visual for students, particularly Class 11 Science learners.

### Approach

- Built the web experience using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

A built 3D physics-learning website. The supplied record describes the project as previously unhosted.

## Autonomous Driving Robot

[Project notes](/projects/autonomous-robot/)

**Status:** Architecture exploration

Exploring the bridge from perception to movement, through a proposed two-board autonomous robot architecture.

**Purpose:** The RoboFest Gujarat 5.0 Junior AI Autonomous Driving Bots category prompted a design for lane following, obstacle avoidance, and traffic-signal response.

**Technologies / scope:** Raspberry Pi 3, Raspberry Pi 4, SPI, IR / ultrasonic sensors, L298N

### Approach

- Proposed separating camera and AI processing on Raspberry Pi 4 from sensor and motor control on Raspberry Pi 3.
- Planned SPI communication between the boards.
- Considered cameras, IR and ultrasonic sensors, an L298N motor driver, and DC geared motors within competition constraints.

### Outcome

Robot architecture and component planning. No completed autonomous test performance or competition result is confirmed.

### Documented limits

- A fully assembled robot, implemented perception method, and working board communication are not verified.
- No award, rank, or qualification is claimed.
